Gutter is a comic reader for the comics you already keep on your own server.

Connect to Komga or Kavita with your username and password or an API key, or to a self-hosted Gutter server. Your library shows up with series, covers and where you left off.

READING
• Page mode or vertical scroll mode
• Left-to-right or right-to-left, for manga
• Fit to screen, width or height
• Slide, fade or instant page turns
• Choice of tap zone layouts, page background and brightness
• Optional page turns with the volume buttons
• Sleep timer for reading in bed

YOUR LIBRARY
• Searchable library with filters and sorting
• Reading queues to plan what to read next
• Progress syncs both ways with your server
• Your library stays browsable when the server is out of reach

GUTTER PRO
A one-time purchase, not a subscription. You will never pay for a version upgrade: one purchase covers every future version.
• Offline downloads: take issues with you
• Guided zoom: step through a page panel by panel (panels are found on your device, so it works with Komga and Kavita), and through two-page spreads. You can try panel zoom on one issue for free
• More than one server
• Reading stats: pages per day, streaks and more

Reading, streaming and progress sync are free.

PRIVACY
Gutter collects nothing. No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. It talks only to the servers you add.

Gutter does not include or sell comics. You need your own Komga, Kavita or Gutter server. The Gutter server is free and open source: github.com/rthaines211/gutter-server
