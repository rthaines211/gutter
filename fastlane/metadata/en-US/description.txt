Gutter is a comic reader for the comics you already own: CBZ files on your iPhone or iPad, or the library on your own Komga, Kavita, OPDS or Gutter server. Reading, file import and progress sync are free. Pro is one purchase, never a subscription.

TRY IT IN SECONDS
No comics yet? Tap Try a sample comic on the first screen and you're reading. Have CBZ files? Tap Import comics from Files and pick them. No account, nothing to set up.

BRING YOUR SERVER
Connect to Komga or Kavita with your username and password or an API key, to any OPDS catalog with page streaming (OPDS-PSE), or to a self-hosted Gutter server. Your series, covers and place in every issue show up. With Komga, Kavita or a Gutter server, progress syncs both ways.

READING
• Page mode or vertical scroll mode
• Left-to-right or right-to-left, for manga
• Fit to screen, width or height
• Slide, fade or instant page turns
• Choice of tap zone layouts, page background and brightness
• Optional page turns with the volume buttons
• Sleep timer for reading in bed
• On iPad, tap the comic's title while reading to see its series in a side panel, without leaving the page

YOUR LIBRARY
• Pick up where you left off from the Read tab
• Searchable library with filters and sorting
• Tap the circle on any issue to mark it read or unread, with Undo
• Reading queues to plan what to read next
• Your library stays browsable when the server is out of reach

GUTTER PRO
One purchase covers every future version. No subscription, ever.
• Guided zoom: step through a page panel by panel, and through two-page spreads. Panels are found on your device, so it works with Komga and Kavita too. Your first comic opens with it on, free for that issue
• Offline downloads: take issues from your server with you
• More than one server
• Reading stats: pages per day, streaks and more, with a card you can share. The card shows your numbers, never what you read

PRIVACY
Gutter collects nothing. No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking. It talks only to the servers you add.

Gutter does not include or sell comics. Apart from the free sample, you need your own CBZ files, or your own Komga, Kavita, OPDS or Gutter server. The Gutter server is free and open source: github.com/rthaines211/gutter-server
