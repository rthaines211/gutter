Gutter is a comic reader for people who run their own comic server. It has no content of its own and no accounts with us. On first launch it asks for the address of the user's server and their login.

Demo server (public-domain comics, read-only reviewer account):
- Server type: Komga (selected by default)
- Address: TODO_DEMO_SERVER_URL
- Username: TODO_DEMO_USERNAME
- Password: TODO_DEMO_PASSWORD
(Also enter the username and password in the Sign-In Information fields.)

Steps: open the app, keep Komga selected, enter the address, username and password, then tap Connect. The library loads. Tap any series, then an issue to read. Swipe or tap the page edges to turn pages. Swipe up for the reader controls.

In-app purchase: "Gutter Pro" is a one-time, non-consumable unlock ($12.99). It adds offline downloads, guided zoom (panel by panel, with panels found on the device, and through two-page spreads), more than one server, and reading stats. To see it: tap the download icon on any issue, or Settings > Gutter Pro. Restore Purchases is on the same sheet. Reading, streaming and progress sync are free.

Volume buttons (2.5.9): turning pages with the volume buttons is off by default. Users can turn it on under the reader's Aa > More. When on, the buttons turn pages only while a comic is open.

Network security: the app connects only to servers the user types in, which are usually on their home network or behind their own reverse proxy, often over plain HTTP. That is why App Transport Security allows arbitrary loads. The app talks to no other servers.

Privacy: no data is collected. No analytics, ads or tracking. Settings, progress and downloads stay on the device and the user's own server.
